This one is honest by omission: I made the project, copied the assets across from the platformer I'd just finished, and never wrote a line of it.

What exists is a Godot project with a single empty scene in it. No player, no platforms, no scrolling, no scoring. The plan was the obvious one — a character that bounces continuously, procedurally generated platforms rising past, a camera that follows your best height, and a score that's just how far up you got.

It was started the same day I finished the adventure platformer, which I think explains it. Finishing something makes starting the next thing feel free, and it isn't.

I'll come back to it once I've finished the other things I'm building. Until then it stays here, marked in progress, because that's what it is.
