There is a circuit breaker in the hallway and it is ugly. That is the entire origin of this project.

The makerspace had a wooden CNC I had never touched, and covering the breaker seemed like a small enough first job that I couldn't ruin much by getting it wrong. I drew Rotom — **[TK: why Rotom? was it just the shape fitting the box, or something else?]**

![The cut profile](./photos/Screenshot%202026-08-21%20at%2010.57.18.png)

The profile does most of the work. The head spike and the lightning-bolt arms come out of the same flat cut, and the rectangular window in the middle sits over the switches, so the breaker still works without taking the cover off. The eyes and the arms were printed separately and added after. Magnets on the back hold it to the breaker door — no screws, nothing permanent, and it lifts off in one motion.
