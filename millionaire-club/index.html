Millionaire Club Frame

complete

A picture frame for the Millionaire Club, 2025.

The frame is three pieces that hold a clear panel of photographs. A printed base spells out MILLIONAIRE CLUB 2025 in dripping letters, wide enough to stand the whole thing up on its own. A shaka hangs off the top corner, drawn as an open lattice rather than a solid hand so it reads as line work instead of a lump of plastic. And the panel itself carries etched outlines over the photographs, so the artwork sits in front of the images without hiding them.

Almost all the work is in the lettering. Drippy display type is fun to draw and awkward to manufacture: every letter has to stay connected to the base bar, the enclosed counters in the Os and the B have to survive as holes rather than falling out, and the drips need enough thickness at their thinnest point to not snap when handled. The shaka has the same problem in a different shape — an open web of strands is easy to draw and easy to break.

You can open the CAD on Onshape and pull the parts apart.
